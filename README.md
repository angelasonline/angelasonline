# Angela Johnson

I explore what happens when communication, language, and intelligence are treated as infrastructure. Who controls the infrastructure people rely on to communicate, understand, and participate, and what happens when that infrastructure fails?

### Projects

- **[LangLayer](https://github.com/angelasonline/langlayer)**  
  A language layer for the internet. Makes AI and language services portable across models, devices, and networks.

- **[Broadcast Layer](https://github.com/angelasonline/broadcast-layer)**  
  Real-time captions and sign language as broadcast infrastructure, designed for reliable, accessible communication.

- **[Buzz Bridge](https://github.com/angelasonline/buzz-bridge)**  
  Connects AI agent systems to public communication channels and networks.

- **[Block Party Kit](https://github.com/angelasonline/blockparty-kit)**  
  A community-owned approach to running AI locally across a room full of ordinary computers.

### The idea

Models will change. The infrastructure for accessing and collaborating with them should outlast any one model.
